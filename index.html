LYCEA

The fleet's school

Named after the Lyceum, Aristotle's school, which had its own gymnasium: learning and training in one place. A Unity project where the DREAM Robotics robots learn in simulation before trying it for real, taught with Unity ML-Agents: WHIP to walk, and NORA to drive without hitting things and to follow a line.

Overview

Learn in simulation, then for real

Modules

Parts like the real ones

Ultrasonic sensors, line sensors, servos, mecanum wheels and an IMU, each written to behave like the part on the robot, down to the firmware's “no echo” value.

Robots

NORA and WHIP

NORA at her real size and weight with her sensors where the real ones are; WHIP as a hexapod with 18 servos, an MPU6050 and a sonar at the front.

Arena

A room that changes

A 4 × 4 m room with walls, boxes that move every episode, and optionally a black-tape oval for line following. Six arenas train side by side.

Agents

Three behaviours

NORA roams without touching anything and follows the tape loop; WHIP walks forwards, staying upright, without thrashing.

Self-test

Checked before training

LYCEA › Self-test passes: NORA's sonar reads a wall at the right distance, her line sensors see tape, she drives and strafes straight, and WHIP stands level on his servos.

Next

Onto the real robots

The inputs are the real sensor readings, so a trained NORA policy could run on the PC and drive her over WiFi. Exact models next, through the URDF Importer.

Status: compiles in Unity 6000.6.2f1 with ML-Agents 4.1.0, the three training scenes are built and the self-test passes. Training itself hasn't been run yet.

Training

From an empty scene to a model

Build a scene in Unity (LYCEA › Build NORA arena, line following, or WHIP), then train it. The first run sets up Python 3.10 and mlagents 1.1.0 with uv. Settings are in config/*.yaml (PPO).

> train.bat NoraAvoid       # or NoraLine, WhipWalk
# when it says it's listening, press Play in Unity
# the model lands in results\NoraAvoid\NoraAvoid.onnx:
# drop it on the agent's Behavior Parameters › Model
> train.bat NoraAvoid       # again: resumes where it stopped
